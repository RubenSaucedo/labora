# Resume writing

Resume writing is selection under constraints. The goal is a document a reader can understand quickly, with wording the person recognises as true.

## Core rules

- Write what the person confirms. Use their words where they already have good ones.
- When detail is vague, ask a specific question instead of inserting a placeholder.
- When you propose wording they have not confirmed, say so and let them accept, edit, or reject it.
- Never silently widen scope, ownership, seniority, adoption, production status, or a number.
- Prefer section fit, clear accomplishment arcs, and visible proof over keyword stuffing.
- A gap is a next question or a choice, never a verdict.

## Section missions

- **Headline:** name the professional scope the person wants to be interviewed on.
- **Summary:** interpret the career: identity, level, current scope, and one differentiator when confirmed.
- **Experience:** prove scope, ownership, judgment, consequence, and progression.
- **Technical Skills:** provide a compact retrieval index of accurate terms.
- **Projects:** show externally inspectable or otherwise distinctive implementation proof.
- **Education, certifications, awards:** carry formal credentials and recognitions without implying production experience they do not state.

A true statement in the wrong section is still an editorial defect. Move or delete before polishing.
